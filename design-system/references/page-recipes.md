# Page Recipes

## Portfolio home — archive of useful systems

1. Quiet chrome
2. Editorial hero plus one system artifact
3. Current state panel
4. Asymmetric proof-card grid
5. Trajectory rows
6. Three principles
7. Direct contact close

The home page must orient first and persuade second. Keep the reading path under seven sections.

## Product landing page — one friction, one changed behavior

1. Product promise with an honest status tag
2. The friction, stated in the user's words
3. The product loop: input → human review → result
4. Two or three proof points or screen artifacts
5. Privacy or trust boundary where relevant
6. One action

For AI products, visualise where the human reviews, edits, or decides. Never hide it in footnotes.

## Case study — decision record

1. Scope, role, and actual status
2. The constrained problem
3. What was built and why this architecture or interaction
4. Evidence: artifact, before/after, test, or tradeoff
5. What changed and what remains open

Make limitations visible. A credible tradeoff earns more trust than a polished fiction.

## Utility interface — calm operational clarity

1. Current state and main action
2. Explainable controls, not magical automation
3. Review surface before a consequential change
4. A recoverable outcome and visible history where it helps

Operational UI favors data and signal modes, dense but calm type, and status that can be read at a glance.
